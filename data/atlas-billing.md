# Atlas Platform — Billing and Refunds

Atlas has three subscription plans. Starter is 29 US dollars per month per workspace and includes 5 seats. Team is 99 US dollars per month per workspace and includes 20 seats. Enterprise is quoted annually and includes single sign-on, an audit log export, and a dedicated support channel. Seats beyond the included count are billed at 6 US dollars per seat per month on Starter and 5 US dollars on Team.

Refunds are granted within 30 days of the charge and only for unused subscription time. The refund is calculated as the prorated value of the remaining days in the billing period, minus any discount that was applied to the original invoice. Refunds for annual Enterprise contracts are handled by the account manager and require a written amendment to the order form.

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated: the customer pays the difference between the old and the new plan for the remaining days in the current period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period and are never refunded. Changing the billing period from monthly to annual applies a 20 percent discount to the annual price and starts a new 12 month term on the day of the change.

When a payment fails the account enters the dunning process. Atlas retries the card three times over 14 days: on day 1, day 4 and day 11. The workspace stays fully usable while dunning is in progress and the billing owner receives an email before every retry. If all three retries fail the workspace is suspended on day 21 and read-only access is kept for 30 days so the customer can export their data.

Every workspace can download invoices as PDF from the billing page, and invoices include the VAT identifier when the customer has supplied one. VAT is charged according to the billing country on the account, not the country of the card. Customers in the European Union with a valid VAT number are billed with the reverse charge mechanism and the invoice shows 0 percent VAT.

A charge that the customer does not recognise must be investigated by the billing team within 2 business days. If the charge came from an account that the customer owns, billing explains the invoice lines. If the charge is fraudulent the payment is refunded in full and the card is blocked in the payment provider.

Subscription credits such as promotional credits are applied before any card charge and expire after 12 months. Credits are never refunded in cash and are not transferable between workspaces.
